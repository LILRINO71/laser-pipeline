Laser Station Demo

From a Part Studio to the school laser in one click. This page stands in for the Part Studio; the Laser Station panel is the real add-on panel, in demo mode, so nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. 1 Click the plate's top face
  2. 2 Team, then Send to laser
  3. 3 Load it at the laser PC
Robot V2 · Frame Plates Side Plate -Y · 6.35 mm plywood
Click the top face of the plate. Drag to turn it, scroll to zoom.
flat faces (click the one facing up) edges and holes (refused)

Step 3, at the laser PC

  1. Open Laser Station. The blue button says Jobs sent from Onshape: 1 waiting.
  2. Click it, then Load selected. The plate lands on the map of the wood already in the laser, with the face you clicked facing up.
  3. Check the layout (drag a part to move it), then FIRE. Laser Station sends the job to the laser and presses Start.

Try it without a laser: install Laser Station and open the Laser Station DEMO icon. FIRE saves the laser file as a PDF and shows the laser cutting on screen.

Source on GitHub Part: Side Plate -Y from the team's public Robot V2 Onshape document.
